## Refund and cancellation policy

Bhavano is a product of Finfolia Technologies LLP.

**What this covers.** Paid features bought on the Bhavano website or Android app: Featured ads, Instant Alerts, plans and contact
credits.

**When they start.** Each is a digital service that starts when your payment is confirmed. Once it has started, the payment is not
refundable, including when you sell or rent the property, close your ad, or do not use the whole period or all credits.

**When we refund.** We refund the amount you paid if:
- you were charged more than once for the same purchase;
- you were charged and the service did not start, and we cannot start it;
- we removed your ad or ended the service by our own mistake.

We do not refund when an ad or account is removed for breaking our Terms of Service.

**How to ask.** Write to us through the Contact page within 7 days of the charge, with the email or phone number on your account and
the payment date and amount.

**How you are paid back.** To the original payment method. We start an approved refund within 7 working days; your bank may take longer
to show it. For payments made in another currency, your bank's exchange rate and fees may make the amount differ.

**Cancellation.** Purchases on the website are one-time payments and do not renew automatically. Subscriptions bought in the iPhone app
renew through Apple: cancel them in your Apple ID settings, and ask Apple for refunds on them.

## Delivery (shipping) policy

Bhavano sells only digital services. Nothing is shipped. A paid feature is added to your ad or account online, usually within a few
minutes of the payment being confirmed. If it has not appeared within 24 hours, write to us through the Contact page.
