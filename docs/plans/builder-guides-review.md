# Review sheet: facts to check before the builder guides are published

Every builder entry is one line saying where the builder is based and what it builds. Nothing else
(no figures, awards, rankings, delivery or financial claims). Check each line, correct or delete it
in `packages/types/src/builderGuides.ts`, and tick the city when done.

**Evidence level:** *A* = widely known company, written from general knowledge; *B* = a smaller or
local company, from general knowledge only; *C* = the only support found was the builder's own page
or a property-portal listing (self-reported).

## Also check
- The RERA authority named for each city (Karnataka K-RERA, MahaRERA, Delhi/Haryana/UP for NCR,
  Telangana TG RERA, TNRERA, WBRERA, GujRERA, Rajasthan RERA, UP RERA) is the right name today.
- The city-specific paragraph for each city is accurate (for example, which bodies approve plans).
- That you are comfortable naming each company at all. Removing a name is the safest correction.
- Companies in dispute, insolvency or under regulatory action were left out on purpose (not
  researched in depth). If you know of any on the list, remove them.
