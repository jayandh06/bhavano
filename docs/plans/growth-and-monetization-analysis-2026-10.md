## 2. Diagnosis
1. **Paid acquisition does not pay back at today's monetization.** About ₹80K a month buys roughly 55 paying users in October: about ₹1,450 per payer, against about ₹220 of revenue per payer in the month. Even with repeat purchases the payback is years. Cost per *post* looks fine (about ₹190), but a post earns about ₹15.
2. **Supply outruns demand.** 1,224 listings, 60 enquiries a month. Owners who post and get nothing do not repeat (6% repeat) or buy Featured twice, and "Featured gets more views" is hard to sell when views convert to little. Demand is the bottleneck, not supply.
3. **Monetization is one-off and thin:** Featured and publish only. Slot packs, Agent Pro, Plus and contact credits have sold about nothing. Nothing recurring.
4. **44% of inventory is bulk-imported listings that earn only if claimed.** Claim rate is not measured yet.
5. **Seeker ads are an expensive way to buy demand:** ₹7K in a month for 4 enquiries.

## 3. Plan, in order of value
**A. Fix unit economics now (this week, saves money, no risk to organic):**
- Put a payback rule on Google Ads: spend only where cost per paying user is under about ₹600 (first-purchase revenue times 2 to 3). Cut the worst cities and keywords, keep Generic, Sell and Rent Out winners. Target spend ₹40 to 50K a month while the rest is fixed; this alone moves revenue to about 25 to 30% of spend.
- Report weekly: cost per paying user, post-to-paid rate, enquiries per live listing, claim rate, repeat posters, organic share of sign-ups.

**B. Make owners get answers (liquidity), the biggest lever on retention and Featured sales:**
- Alert existing seekers: saved searches, WhatsApp and push when a matching ad appears (the machinery exists; the volume of seekers is the gap). Cheap demand: SEO, WhatsApp share, referrals.
- Show owners proof ("people viewed / saved / revealed your number") and nudge slow responses. Target: 3.7% of listings with an enquiry to 15%.

**C. Raise revenue per owner:**
- Lift post-to-Featured from about 14%: offer the Featured upsell at the highest-intent moment (already built), add outcome proof, and a time-limited bundle.
- Sell **recurring plans to operators and brokers** instead of one-off boosts: PG, coworking, storage and agents (Agent Pro, lead packs). You already hold their listings from the bulk import. One operator on a ₹500 to 1,500 monthly plan equals 3 to 6 Featured sales a month. Start by calling the 20 most active operators.
- Monetize demand: contact reveals are free (163 a month); a small paid pack after the free quota, and Bhavano Plus alerts. Test prices on a small share.

**D. Lower the cost of getting owners:**
- Organic and AI answers are growing and cheap: keep publishing locality and "how to" content, finish the indexing cleanup (done), and track ChatGPT and Google organic sign-ups (38% and 24% sign in against 1% for direct).
- WhatsApp-first posting (`whatsapp-conversational-posting.md`): free replies inside the 24-hour window, no OTP step.
- Referral program and broker outreach; claim campaigns for the bulk-imported listings.

**E. Housekeeping that protects the data:** the 51,809 "direct" visits (1% sign in) are probably automated or untagged; judge traffic by JS-confirmed human visits.

## 4. 90-day targets (suggested)
Spend ₹40 to 50K a month; revenue ₹40K+ a month (from ₹12K) with at least 10 recurring operator or broker accounts; enquiries per month 60 to 300; repeat posters 6% to 15%; organic sign-ups at least a third of all sign-ups.

## 5. Unknowns to measure first
Claim rate of bulk-imported listings; paying users by acquisition source; revenue per cohort; true cost per paying user by campaign.
