# Ideas: selling monthly plans to PG and coworking operators (2026-10-27)

## Where it stands (production, 2026-10-27)
- **PG:** 283 live, 243 imported (10 bulk-import accounts, unclaimed) and 40 posted by 39 real operators. **Coworking:** 296 live, 293 imported and 3 posted by operators.
  So about 43 real operators have joined; the rest is claimable inventory (Bengaluru coworking 110, Delhi NCR PG 64, Chennai coworking 59 and PG 33).
- **Demand delivered in the last 30 days:** PG 2 enquiries and 10 contact reveals across 283 listings; coworking 1 enquiry and 1 reveal across 296. Apartments and houses get 37 enquiries and 139 reveals.
  Average views per PG or coworking listing: 4 to 7. **A plan is bought for leads; today Bhavano delivers almost none to this segment.**
- **Current plans do not fit them:** the slot pack (₹500 a month, 10 slots) and Agent Pro (₹1,000 a month per unit, +20 slots) sell *more listing slots*, but a PG or coworking operator has one to three properties and 3 free slots.
  Featured costs ₹449 to ₹699 for 30 days one-off.

## Plan shape: "Operator" plan per property (suggested ₹499 to 999 a month)
Included: Featured placement on the PG or coworking pages of its locality, instant WhatsApp alerts for every enquiry and number reveal (Instant Alerts already ships with Featured), a vacancy or availability line ("3 beds left", "day pass available")
kept fresh by a monthly reminder, an operator profile page listing all its properties, and a **weekly WhatsApp report** (views, reveals, enquiries). Multi-location coworking operators: a chain price per location after the third.

## Ways to sell it
1. **Trial at claim time.** The claim message (435 sent in 30 days) already reaches operators. Give 14 days of the Operator plan free on claim, send the weekly report on days 3, 7 and 10, ask to continue on day 10 with the numbers in the message.
2. **Subscription Featured.** "Featured every month, auto-renewed, ₹399 against ₹449 to ₹699 one-off", the cheapest step from today's product.
3. **Pay per lead alternative:** ₹30 to 50 per verified enquiry or reveal for PGs (a bed is worth ₹6 to 10K a month), ₹300 to 1,000 per qualified tour request for coworking. Operators who fear a flat fee often accept this; it also proves demand honestly.
4. **Founding operators:** the first 20 get the price locked for 12 months. Referral: a free month for every operator they bring.
5. **Extension guarantee, not a refund:** "under 5 contact requests in 30 days and we extend 30 days free". Safe only once demand is up; with today's volume it would trigger often.
6. **Chains and associations:** coworking chains and PG owners' groups (Facebook groups, hostel associations, college areas) in one conversation; one yes brings 5 to 20 properties.
7. **Channels:** WhatsApp (claim templates), a call to the 20 most promising operators, the outreach contacts from Google Places (29 Chennai PG contacts, 35 Bengaluru, 53 Delhi NCR), and Facebook groups.

## Order
1. **Create the demand first** (without it nothing sells): locality pages and ads for "PG in <area>", the women's-PG pages and Chennai/Adyar outreach, "coworking day pass" pages, saved-search alerts to existing seekers. Target 20+ enquiries a month for PG and coworking before charging anyone.
2. **Pilot with 20 operators** (Chennai and Adyar PGs, Bengaluru coworking): free trial, weekly reports, then ask. Success: at least 5 paying within 6 weeks at ₹499 or more; track claim rate, trial to paid, churn.
3. Then productize: subscription Featured, the operator profile page and the weekly report job.

## Revenue sanity check
20 operators at ₹500 is ₹10K a month, 100 is ₹50K. Cheap per operator; the segment matters because it is recurring and cheap to reach (already listed, already contactable).
