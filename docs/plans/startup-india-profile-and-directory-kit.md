## Long description (about 150 words)
Bhavano is a classifieds marketplace for property and spaces across India. Tenants, buyers, owners and agents use it to find and list apartments, houses, villas, plots, PG rooms, storage, coworking desks and commercial space, with no login needed to browse.

Owners and agents post their own listings with photos, details and location, and Bhavano checks each listing before it goes live. Seekers can search by city, area and category, save listings, message owners and post a requirement so that owners come to them. A Trust and Safety page explains what we check and what we do not verify.

Bhavano also publishes free guides and calculators, including an EMI calculator, a rent affordability calculator and an area unit converter, to help people make better housing decisions.

Bhavano is a product of Finfolia Technologies LLP.
